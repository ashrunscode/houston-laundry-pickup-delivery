## What to put in the bag, and what to leave out

Everyday washable laundry belongs in the bag: shirts, towels, sheets, kids' clothes, gym clothes, and similar household loads. Read the care label. We do not offer dry cleaning. Garments marked dry-clean only should not go in a wash-and-fold bag. Known infection, substantial blood, or other special precautions are not ordinary household laundry. Keep heavily soiled pieces separate so they are easy to flag. Note bulky items when you book.

Folded laundry comes back ready to put away. Preferences you care about, such as hangers on a few pieces or separating a work uniform, belong in the order notes. We weigh the load at intake. The published per-pound rate is what the scale is multiplied by, not a guess from the photo of the bag.
