## What belongs in a wash and fold bag

- T-shirts, jeans, socks, underwear, towels, and sheets with a normal wash label
- Kids' everyday clothes
- Work clothes that are machine washable, noted in the order if you want them grouped

Leave out dry-clean-only garments. We do not offer dry cleaning. Leave out items with a care label you are not willing to follow.
